Thesis: Combine Large Language Model with Logical Symbolic Solver for multi-hop Problem in RAG

Welcome to our thesis project, the Law-Logic App, where we merge the precision of legal reasoning with the capabilities of AI technology. Our project focuses on developing an innovative platform that integrates advanced natural language processing and logical reasoning to enhance legal analysis and decision-making processes. The Law-Logic App aims to assist legal professionals by providing tools for efficient research, document analysis, and case preparation. By leveraging AI algorithms, our platform can interpret complex legal texts, extract pertinent information, and generate reasoned conclusions to support legal arguments and strategies.

We are students majoring in Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Science, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City, and this is our thesis project in the field of Data Science. Through this thesis project, we explore the intersection of law and technology, striving to improve accessibility to legal information and promote efficiency in legal practice. Our goal is to contribute to the evolution of legal technology by offering a robust and user-friendly solution that empowers users with enhanced analytical capabilities. Join us on our journey as we innovate and redefine the future of legal analysis with the Law-Logic App.
