Question:
Are stocks considered an asset?
###
Answer:
Yes, stocks are considered an asset according to the provided legal context. Here’s the reasoning:

Article 105 of the 2015 Civil Code outlines what constitutes property.
It specifies that property includes objects, money, valuable papers, and property rights.
Within the category of valuable papers, it explicitly mentions "shares" (which are synonymous with stocks).
Thus, stocks fall under the category of valuable papers and are therefore considered property under the law. Consequently, stocks are an asset.
